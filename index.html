Department of Dravyaguna
Vata
Botanical Name :

  • Ficus benghalensis Linn

    • Family Name :

  • Moraceae

    • Local Name :

  • Aladamara

    • Vernacular Name :

  • Hindi –Vata

  • English – Banyan tree

    • Synonyms :

  • Vat

  • Banyan tree


    • Habitat :

  • Ficus bengalensis is cultivated throughout India and very common in Bihar, Odissa, West bengal, Karnataka, Tamilnadu and Kerala


    • External Morphology :

  • Leaves : Simple,alternate,petiolate,15 cm long and 10 cm wide

  • Flowers : Sessile, unisexeual, monoecious,minute situated on the inner walls of a fleshy recepticular cavity with an apical opening guarded by scales

  • Fruit : Syconus


    • Useful Parts :

  • Twaka(Stem bark), Kshira(Latex), Patra(Leaf)


    • Rasadi Panchaka :

  • Rasa - Kasaya

  • Guna - Guru Ruksa

  • Vipaka - Katu

  • Virya - Sita


    • Pharmalogical Action :

  • Hyupoglycaemic

  • Hypotensive

  • Antidiabetic


    • Therapeutic Administration:

  • Mukhadusika : Paste prepared from Tender leaves of vata and pulp of narikela is applied over the face in case of Mukhadusika

  • Pradara : Decoction of bark of Nyagrodha is taken with paste of Lodhra internally;otherwise in this mixture has to be kept in Vagina

  • Vrana : Paste of bark of Salmali,root of Bala and tender leaves of Nyagrodha is applied over the Vrana or the decoction of same combination is to be sprinkled


    • Dose

  • Kwatha : 50 to 100 ml

  • Curna (Powder) : 3 to 6 g


    • Formulation:

  • Nyagrodhadi Curna

  • Nyagrodhadya Ghrita

  • Pancavalkala lepa
